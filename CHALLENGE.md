# Eagle Day challenge A: improve a Moroccan learning experience

## The problem

A fictional pharmacy learner in Casablanca wants to communicate clearly with customers who may read French and prefer some explanations orally in darija. The current journey supports preparation, a guided dialogue, immediate feedback, short learning checks and saved progress.

Choose one bounded improvement and make the experience more useful, clear or accessible. Keep people and dialogue content synthetic. This communication exercise does not provide health advice.

## Choose one track

### A1: Audit and repair

Find one frustrating point in the learner journey. Explain the learner's task, show the problem, implement the fix and test the revised path with another person.

### A2: Add a useful interaction

Add one interaction that helps a multilingual learner practise, understand feedback, recover work or use the experience on mobile. Include the relevant empty, progress and error states.

### A3: Redesign a complete path

Redesign one full learner path for the audience. Preserve a clear learning goal and demonstrate working behavior across the complete path.

## Deliverables

- A short problem statement naming the learner, task and pain point.
- At least two relevant product references and the pattern you selected.
- Before and after captures.
- A working implementation of one bounded path.
- Keyboard and mobile checks.
- One test with another person, including what changed after the test.
- A concise list of assumptions and remaining gaps.
- Source and setup instructions.

## Working principles

Use synthetic people and dialogue. Keep the interface understandable in French and useful in a multilingual Moroccan context. If an example raises a precise health question, route it to a pharmacist instead of answering it inside the exercise.
