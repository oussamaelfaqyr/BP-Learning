# Eagle Day participant guide

Date: 19 September 2026. Event timezone: Africa/Casablanca.

Choose one challenge and one bounded outcome. Start with the person who must use the system. Walk the current journey, record a baseline, name one user problem and define a visible success check. Research at least two relevant product patterns. Select one interaction and explain why it fits this audience.

## Choose a track

**BP learning platform:** improve one learner-facing journey for Moroccan pharmacy employees in a French-language practice scenario. Audit and fix a frustrating path, add one missing interaction, or redesign one bounded journey.

**Motor insurance:** improve a browser-only claims journey for a Moroccan motorist under stress or the broker employee handling the call. Test the interface with fictional cases: a minor Casablanca collision with a missing joint report, an immobilised vehicle near Rabat with unverified assistance, an injury case in Marrakech requiring human escalation, conflicting policy details, or a duplicate import. The prototype does not decide liability, coverage or payout.

**Optional browser-agent bonus:** with explicit authorization, demonstrate public test-account signup, owner-handled consent, OTP or CAPTCHA, login, minimal visible-field reading, source-aware mapping and a human-confirmed local merge. Do not buy insurance, submit a claim, contact a provider, store credentials or use a hidden API.

Build one complete path before adding breadth. Include the relevant loading, empty, error and recovery states. Check keyboard use, mobile use, language, feedback and whether work is preserved. Test with another person. Keep one failed attempt and the correction.

## Review criteria

Show how your decisions improve the user’s experience, supported by a working demo and test evidence. Reviewers look for client understanding, research and judgement, detail and usability, creative interaction, working implementation and adaptation. Creative interaction must help this audience complete a difficult task; novelty alone is insufficient.

Your handoff includes a working demo, source without secrets or dependency caches, setup instructions, the problem and audience, before and after screenshots, two reference links with reasoning, test notes and a list of mocks or known gaps.

Seated firesides begin at 17:30 Casablanca local time and are capped at ten minutes, using the same format as yesterday. Organisers will confirm the running order, rooms, reviewers and end time.
